Competition Word Problems.

The word problems that competitive examinations set, type by type — each one made from its answer, so the numbers always come out.

Eleven chapters: ages, alligation and mixture, HCF and LCM, ratio, algebraic fractions, systems of equations, sequence and series, arrangements and selections, probability trees, percentages and remainders. Every type opens with one worked in full, and the answer key gives the method as well as the answer. The same bench builds the Maths Workbook, the Algebra Workbook and the Statistics Workbook.
