Dividing and Remainders.

A pile of things and a pencil. Ring the groups, count what is left over, and write the sentence underneath — with every part named, so the divisor and the remainder are things you can point at. Then the bit left over turns into a fraction, and the fraction bars turn that into a mixed number and an improper fraction.

Built for reinforcement — do the step, say the word, do it again. The same bench builds the Place Value Workbook.
