Writing maths on screen

A guide for students answering papers on Prep Portal. You type the maths the way you would say it, and the box sets it properly, the way it looks in your textbook.

1 Opening the maths box

  1. Click in the answer where the maths should go.
  2. Press Alt + =. A small box opens with a preview under it.
  3. Type the maths, the way it is said. The preview shows what you will get.
  4. Press Enter to put it in your answer. Esc closes the box without it.
  5. To change it later, double-click the formula. It opens again with what you typed.

Words outside the box are written in handwriting, the way your answers look on paper. Keep the box for maths.

The trap The slash takes only what came since the last plus or minus. For x plus one ALL over two, put brackets round the top: (x + 1)/2. The brackets disappear once it is set.
