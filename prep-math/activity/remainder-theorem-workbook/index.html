Remainder Theorem Workbook.

One method, split into steps small enough to finish, and drawn on the page so nobody has to hold it in their head. Find the zero of the bracket. Swap x for it, a row at a time. Then both together, in a four-step frame that never changes.

Built for reinforcement — say the word, do the step, do it again. The same bench builds the Place Value Workbook.
