Two Scales.

Two scales, two equations. Solve what you can on one scale, then carry what it says a letter is worth over to the other and swap it in.

The four moves

  • Across. Tap a block (or drag it to the other pan of its own scale): it crosses over, and its sign turns over with it.
  • Together. Drag a block onto another in the same pan: numbers add up, and a block and its opposite leave nothing.
  • Share. When both pans cut into equal helpings, share them and keep one helping each side.
  • Swap. When a scale has a letter on its own, it says what that letter is worth: drag its chip onto the same letter on the other scale.

How hard

Start where substitution is the whole job, and work up to a pair of equations with two unknowns.
