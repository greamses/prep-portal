Maths Workbook.

Four families on one sheet, in the order they are taught. What a number is, made of blocks and read off a chart. Adding and taking away, with and without regrouping, written across the page and down it. Dividing by grouping things you can count, with the divisor and the remainder named. And fractions — coloured in, read off, and added when the parts match.

Tick what you need and it comes out as one paper with one code at the foot of it. The same blocks are on the Manipulatives canvas if you would rather move them about than print them.
