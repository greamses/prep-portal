How to Play
  • Flap: tap the sky, or press Space or the up arrow.
  • Choosing: tap an answer, or press 1 to 4. A wrong one ends the flight.
  • Typing: type the answer after the equals sign. The next sum comes as soon as it is right.
  • Every right answer is a point. Fly as long as you can.
  • It beeps. Press the speaker in the corner of the sky to play in silence.
