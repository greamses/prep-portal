Star-nosed mole: Nose with 22 pink tentacles that feel faster than eyes

Animal Facts Grade 2
5 Nov 2026 2 min read

1. The Hook

Whoa! Did you know there’s a tiny mammal whose nose can “see” faster than your eyes can blink? Imagine feeling the world at lightning speed – that’s the star‑nosed mole!

2. Meet the Animal

The star‑nosed mole is a small, blind creature about the size of a hand. It lives in soggy marshes, riverbanks, and swampy fields – think of the wetlands around the Niger Delta. Its most eye‑catching feature is a star‑shaped nose with 22 pink, fleshy “tentacles.” These tentacles are packed with thousands of sensory receptors, making the mole a true underground superhero.

3. The Amazing Ability

When the mole scoots through mud, each tentacle works like a tiny radar dish, sending out electric signals that bounce back from every particle it touches. The brain processes these signals in just 8 milliseconds – faster than a blink! That’s like a gamer reacting to a surprise attack before the screen even changes.

Because the mole can’t see, its nose becomes its eyes, ears, and hands all at once. It can detect a worm moving a few millimetres away, feel the texture of a leaf, and even sense water currents. Imagine playing football and knowing exactly where the ball will be a split second before it arrives – that’s the mole’s “feel‑fast” power.

Each of the 22 tentacles moves independently, sweeping the ground in a 360‑degree sweep. The mole’s brain compares the tiny differences between the signals, creating a detailed “map” of its surroundings. It can grab prey with pinpoint accuracy, snapping it up before the prey even realizes it’s there!

Scientists call this the fastest known tactile detection in the animal kingdom. If you timed it, the mole’s nose would register a touch faster than a cheetah can sprint a short burst – pure “blink‑and‑you‑miss‑it” speed!

4. Creature Power Breakdown

If you had the star‑nosed mole’s nose, you could win any video‑game “reflex” challenge, dodge a soccer ball before it even leaves the kicker’s foot, or find hidden treasures in a sandpit without even looking. Imagine being the ultimate hide‑and‑seek champion, feeling every corner of the room instantly!
