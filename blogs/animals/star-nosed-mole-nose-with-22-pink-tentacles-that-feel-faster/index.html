Meet the Star-Nosed Mole

The star-nosed mole is a small black animal. It spends its life digging deep underground. Its eyes are tiny because underground caves stay completely dark. Instead of big eyes, it has a strange nose shaped like a pink star. This special star nose helps it feel everything inside the dark wet soil.

"

The star-nosed mole has 22 pink tentacles on its nose that feel the dark dirt like tiny hands!

The Ultimate Creature Power

This animal has the fastest sense of touch on Earth. Its nose moves faster than a thumb tapping a game on a smartphone screen! When it touches a tasty worm, it grabs it faster than a danfo bus stopping for a passenger. The 22 pink tentacles work together to give it super touch in total darkness. It can even smell food underwater by blowing air bubbles! That is pure creature power energy.

  • Touch Spots - Thousands of tiny bumps on the nose that feel the ground.
  • Paddle Paws - Big front feet built like shovels for quick digging.
  • Bubble Smelling - Blowing bubbles underwater to catch smells in ponds.
How Super Touch Saves Its Life

Muddy tunnels do not have any room for light. The mole cannot see worms with its small eyes. Its star nose acts like a fast flashlight made of touch. It touches many spots in one second to stay full and strong. Without this fast nose, the mole would go hungry in the dark.

  1. Step 1 - Fast Touch.
    The 22 pink tentacles wiggle and feel the mud.
  2. Step 2 - Quick Choice.
    The brain learns if the item is a worm or a rock.
  3. Step 3 - Snack Attack!
    The mole swallows the food in a split second.

Try This

The Blindfold Touch Challenge. Put five small things inside a school bag, like an eraser, a spoon, a pencil, a coin, and a small toy. Tie a clean cloth around your eyes. Use only your fingertips to feel inside the bag and guess each object as fast as you can. This is how the star-nosed mole sees its world every day!
