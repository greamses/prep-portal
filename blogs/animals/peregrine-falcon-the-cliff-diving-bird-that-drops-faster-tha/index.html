Meet the Animal

The peregrine falcon is a sleek bird with sharp pointy wings. Its feathers are dark on top and white on the belly. It lives on high cliffs, tall towers, and open fields across Africa, including Nigeria. It is famous for its super fast dive called a stoop. Its eyes are huge like a camera lens, helping it see tiny mice from far away.

"

A peregrine falcon can dive faster than a racing car on the Lagos expressway!

How it works

Its secret is a special wing shape that cuts the air like a sharp knife. When it spots food, it climbs high then folds its wings tight. The bird then drops straight down, tucking its body to become a tiny arrow. The fast fall creates a rush of air that pushes the falcon to over 300 km/h. Just like a football player sprinting after a goal, the falcon uses huge leg muscles for strong muscle power. The smooth feathers and powerful muscles work together like a phone’s processor and battery, giving it the power to zoom in a stoop.

  • Aerodynamics - The sleek body lets air flow smoothly, reducing drag.
  • Vision - Its eyes can spot a tiny mouse from 1 km away.
  • Talons - Sharp claws grab the prey in a quick snap.
Why it matters

The fast dive helps the falcon catch quick birds before they escape. It keeps the sky clean by eating pigeons that can spread disease. Farmers love falcons because they reduce the number of insects that eat crops. Its speed also makes it a symbol of courage in many Nigerian stories.
