Amazing Mystery: Tasmanian Tiger

Did you know a tiger that lived on an island looked like a dog? It disappeared over 80 years ago, but we still learn from its tracks.

Meet the Animal

It had stripes like a tiger but a head like a dog. It lived on Tasmania, an island far from Nigeria. It was a marsupial that carried babies in a pouch. It could run fast and jump far. It is now extinct, which means no living ones are left.

"

The thylacine was the only big meat‑eating marsupial left on Earth.

How it works

Its superpower was a silent night hunt. Its ears could hear tiny sounds, like a football crowd far away. Its teeth were sharp like a kitchen knife. It could sprint like a danfo bus racing on a road. Its pouch kept its babies safe, just like a school bag holds books. Scientists call it nocturnal because it was active at night.

  • Stripes - helped hide in grass.
  • Pouch - held its young safely.
  • Sharp teeth - cut meat quickly.
Why it matters

Its hunting skill taught scientists how animals adapt to night life. When it vanished, we learned that losing homes can end a species. Protecting forests now helps other animals stay safe. The thylacine reminds us to keep nature safe for all.
